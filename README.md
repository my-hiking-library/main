# My Hiking Library Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 12, 2026**

My Hiking Library turns hiking workouts from Apple Health into a personal hiking journal. No app-specific account is required. The developer does not operate a server that collects your hiking history, health information, photos, videos, or location.

## Information Used

With your permission, the app accesses:

- **Apple Health:** Completed hiking workouts, dates, duration, distance, elevation, active energy, heart rate, GPS routes, and available workout weather information. Access is read-only; the app does not change Apple Health data.
- **Photos:** Photos, videos, and their date and location metadata to match media with hikes. Photo search analyzes attached hike photos on your device and stores its search index locally. Photos stored in iCloud Photos may need to download for viewing or analysis.
- **Location:** Your current location to show your position on maps.
- **Notifications:** Optional On This Day reminders about past hikes. These may display hike details and a photo on your device’s notification screen.

## Storage and iCloud

Imported workout data, routes, media references, photo-search data, renamed places, and app preferences are stored on your device. The app does not synchronize personal HealthKit information or its photo-search index to iCloud.

Notes, ratings, hidden-hike preferences, and collection names, descriptions, and membership sync through your private iCloud database when available. The app uses an opaque identifier to associate this information with a hike; workout dates, health measurements, routes, locations, and Photo Library identifiers are not included in these synced records.

The developer does not receive or have access to these private iCloud records.

Your photos and videos remain in Apple Photos. My Hiking Library does not upload them; any iCloud Photos syncing is managed separately by Apple.

## Apple Services and Sharing

The app sends a hike’s starting coordinate to Apple’s location and Maps services to identify nearby places. Historical weather requests send the hike’s coordinate and relevant date and time to Apple Weather. These requests do not send the complete workout route, and the developer does not receive them.

Apple services handle information under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

When you choose to share a photo or video, the app provides it to the destination you select through Apple’s share sheet. Shared files may contain embedded metadata, including location. The receiving service’s privacy policy applies.

The app has no ads or third-party tracking or analytics tools. It does not sell personal information or use health, photo, or location information for advertising.

## Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can change Health access in the Health app and Photos, Location, and Notification permissions in iOS Settings. Declining access limits the features that need it. Revoking access does not automatically erase information already stored locally.

You can clear notes, remove ratings, and delete collections within the app. When a source workout is deleted from Apple Health and the app next synchronizes, its local hike and associated synced metadata are removed. Minimal deletion markers may remain in private iCloud storage to prevent older devices from restoring deleted records.

Deleting the app removes its local data but does not delete your original Apple Health workouts, Apple Photos media, or private iCloud records. You can manage the app’s iCloud data through your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings. Synced information may return if you reinstall the app.

Because the developer cannot access your private iCloud records, the developer cannot retrieve or delete them on your behalf.
