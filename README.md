# My Hiking Library Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 1, 2026**

My Hiking Library is a personal hiking journal for iPhone. It uses information from Apple Health to organize and display your hiking history.

## Information the App Accesses

With your permission, My Hiking Library may access:

- **Apple Health:** Hiking workouts, workout dates, duration, distance, elevation, active energy, heart rate, and GPS workout routes.
- **Photo Library:** Photos, dates, and location information used to find photos that may correspond to your hikes.
- **Location:** Your current location for location-based map features.

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time through the Health app or iOS Settings. Some app features may be unavailable without the corresponding permission.

## Health and Location Data

HealthKit-derived information—including workouts, dates, routes, coordinates, distance, duration, elevation, energy, and heart-rate measurements—is stored only on your device.

My Hiking Library does not store personal health information in iCloud. It does not send HealthKit or location data to the developer or to third-party services.

Current-location access is used to provide map functionality. The app does not use your current location for advertising, tracking, or marketing.

## Information Stored in iCloud

The following user-created information may be stored in your private iCloud account so it can be restored and synchronized across your devices:

- Ratings
- Comments and notes
- Collection names and descriptions
- Collection membership
- Opaque identifiers used to associate this information with hikes stored locally on your device

These opaque identifiers do not contain workout measurements, GPS coordinates, dates, routes, heart-rate information, or raw HealthKit workout identifiers.

Renamed trails and areas currently remain on your device and are not synchronized through iCloud.

Your private iCloud data is stored using Apple CloudKit under your Apple Account. The developer does not operate a separate server for this data and does not have access to your private CloudKit records.

## Photos

My Hiking Library does not copy or upload your photographs. Photos remain in your Apple Photos library.

The app stores local references to photos you associate with hikes. These references remain on your device and are used only to display the corresponding photos within the app.

## Data Sharing

My Hiking Library does not:

- Sell or rent personal information
- Share personal information with advertisers or data brokers
- Use HealthKit, Photo Library, or location information for advertising or marketing
- Use third-party analytics or tracking technologies
- Track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites

## Data Retention and Deletion

HealthKit-derived information and other locally stored app data remain on your device until removed by the app or until the app and its local data are deleted.

User-created information synchronized through iCloud may remain in your private iCloud account after deleting the app so it can be restored following reinstallation.

You can delete individual comments, ratings, and collections from within My Hiking Library. You can also manage or delete the app’s stored iCloud data through your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.

Deleting synchronized information may remove it from other devices signed into the same Apple Account.

## Children’s Privacy

My Hiking Library is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The developer does not receive personal information from users of the app.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.
